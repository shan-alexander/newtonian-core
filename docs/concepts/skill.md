# Skill

Tiny reflex the Executive may run. 3T skill layer. Heartbeat, watchdog pulse, enable cancel-on-disconnect, estop poll.

Skills may run on a faster clock than the Executive (L0/L1). Subsumption is allowed **of the Executive**, not **inside the chart**. Skills post beliefs or enqueue messages. They do not call `apply`. They do not live as XOR children.
